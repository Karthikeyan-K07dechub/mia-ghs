What is the Golden Harvest Scheme?

Dreaming in gold, but don't want the upfront payment stress? Our gold investment scheme makes authentic jewellery shopping effortless!

The Golden Harvest Scheme is Mia by Tanishq’s monthly savings plan that makes owning Pinterest-worthy gold jewellery simple, no matter your budget. You simply have to set aside a fixed amount every month for 11 months.

In the 12th month, Mia will add a bonus equal to one full instalment. So, you’ll get to shop for more than what you saved! The best part is that this gold scheme comes with no complex terms or hidden charges. It’s just a simple, trusted way to plan ahead for meaningful gold purchases.

Company Benefit by Purchase Month

Benefit varies by purchase month

The company provides an additional benefit based on the month in which the customer makes the purchase. The benefit is calculated as a percentage of the first month's instalment. Example : ₹10,000 monthly instalment.

Birthday & Anniversary Benefit

Special benefits for special occasions

Customers can avail of a special birthday or wedding anniversary benefit if their own or their spouse's birthday/anniversary falls within 20 days before or after the purchase date.

Is there a penalty for missing a monthly payment with the Mia’s Golden Harvest scheme?

On missing a golden harvest instalment payment for a specific month, you’ll have to pay the pending value in the following month (+that month’s amount). However, upon constantly missing the gold harvest scheme payments, your eligibility for Mia’s bonus will be affected.

Can I cancel the Mia Golden Harvest jewellery purchase plan before maturity?

Please contact your nearest Mia store for the cancellation terms that apply to your plan.

Can I buy diamond or pearl jewellery with the gold investment plan maturity amount?

Please contact your nearest Mia store to confirm which diamond or pearl jewellery is eligible under your plan.
